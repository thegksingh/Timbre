Timbre is a simple yet powerful Text-to-Audio tool designed to convert your written text into natural-sounding speech. It’s perfect for students, content creators, or anyone who wants to turn notes, articles, or scripts into audio quickly and easily.

With Timbre, you can:

Convert any text file into clear, high-quality audio in .mp3 format.

Choose from multiple voices and adjust speech parameters like speed and pitch.

Create podcasts, audio notes, or voiceovers directly from your text files.



---

💡 How It Works

1. Input Text: You provide a .txt file with the text you want to convert.


2. Select Voice: Timbre allows you to choose from a variety of AI voices for your audio output.


3. Adjust Settings: You can customize the speed (rate) and pitch of the speech to make it sound natural and pleasant.


4. Generate Audio: Timbre processes the text using AI-powered Text-to-Speech technology and produces a .mp3 audio file.


5. Output: The generated audio file is saved in the output folder, ready to play or share.




---

📝 What Timbre Does

Text-to-Speech Conversion: Transforms written text into spoken words with high clarity.

Voice Customization: Select different voices and modify speed/pitch for the perfect tone.

Easy to Use: No coding skills are required — just provide the text file and let Timbre do the rest.

Versatile Applications: Ideal for podcasts, audio lectures, study notes, storytelling, or accessibility purposes.



---

⚙️ How to Use

1. Open Timbre.


2. Select the Text-to-Speech (TTS) option.


3. Choose your input text file.


4. Pick a voice from the available options.


5. Adjust the speech speed and pitch if needed.


6. Generate the audio — the resulting .mp3 file will be saved in the output folder.
